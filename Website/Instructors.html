About DoBu

Our Mission

To empower individuals through the art of martial arts — building discipline, confidence, and physical fitness in a welcoming, professional environment.

Our Vision

To be the premier martial arts academy in the UK, producing champions on and off the mat.

Our History

Founded in 2005 by Mauricio Gomez, DoBu Martial Arts began with a single location and a small group of dedicated students. Through an unwavering commitment to technique, discipline, and community, DoBu has grown into one of London's top martial arts academies. Today, we serve hundreds of members, offering world-class coaching across multiple disciplines in state-of-the-art facilities.
